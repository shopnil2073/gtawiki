**Short answer: nobody outside Rockstar knows yet.** GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and Rockstar has not announced a PC version or a date for one.

## What Rockstar and Take-Two have confirmed

- The launch platforms are **PS5 and Xbox Series X|S**.
- No PC version has been announced.
- Take-Two&apos;s CEO has previously said Rockstar has its own approach to platforms and will share news when it is ready.

## Why people expect a PC version

Rockstar&apos;s past games reached PC after their console launches:

| Game | Console launch | PC launch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grand Theft Auto V | September 2013 (PS3, Xbox 360) | April 2015 |
| Red Dead Redemption 2 | October 2018 | November 2019 |

That pattern is a reason for optimism, not a promise. Until Rockstar announces something, treat any PC date you see online as speculation.
