If you picked up GTA 6 on PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X|S this week, or you are a PC player watching from the sidelines, you are probably asking the same thing: **will GTA 6 come to PC after launch**? Here is the honest answer as of November 23, 2026. Rockstar has not announced a PC version, and it has not said it will never make one.

That gap between “not announced” and “not happening” is where most of the confusion lives, and where most of the clickbait gets written. Below you will find what Rockstar and Take-Two have put on the record, what is still rumor, how long PC players waited for Rockstar’s earlier games, and what makes sense to do while you wait.

## <Color value="#ff7a45">The short answer: a GTA 6 PC version is unconfirmed, not ruled out</Color>

Rockstar’s official GTA VI page lists PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S and nothing else. Take-Two’s pre-order announcement on June 24, 2026 named the same two consoles, and the launch platforms in Take-Two’s November 9 earnings materials were unchanged: consoles only.

> **Bottom line for now:** GTA 6 is console-only today. A PC release has not been confirmed, dated or priced, and any specific PC date you read online is a guess.

## <Color value="#ff7a45">What Rockstar and Take-Two have actually said</Color>

### Rockstar’s own listings: consoles only

Rockstar has never said “no PC.” It has simply never said “PC.” Its GTA VI page, the store listings and the launch-day post all name the two consoles. Rockstar is a studio that announces things on its own schedule, so silence is not a denial, but it is not a promise either.

### What Take-Two’s CEO says about PC

Strauss Zelnick runs Take-Two, Rockstar’s parent company, and he gets asked about PC at almost every earnings call. His line has been consistent. In May 2026 he said Rockstar always starts on console, because that is where the core audience for a game like this lives. At a September investor event he said PC is becoming more important to the company over time, without confirming anything for GTA 6. And when Variety asked about additional platforms after the November 9 earnings call, he pointed out that information about new versions comes from the publishing labels, which for GTA means Rockstar.

Read together, that is a clear signal about priorities and no signal at all about dates. Take-Two knows PC matters (Zelnick has said PC can account for a large share, often cited as 40 to 50 percent, of a big modern release’s sales), and it is leaving the announcement to Rockstar.

## <Color value="#ff7a45">Will GTA 6 come to PC after launch? What Rockstar’s history suggests</Color>

The strongest evidence we have is precedent, and Rockstar’s record is consistent: console first, PC later, with a gap that has varied a lot.

| Game | Console launch | PC launch | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grand Theft Auto IV | April 2008 | December 2008 | About 7 months |
| Grand Theft Auto V | September 2013 (PS3, Xbox 360) | April 2015 | About 19 months, and about 5 months after the PS4 and Xbox One versions |
| Red Dead Redemption 2 | October 2018 | November 2019 | About 12 months |

That pattern is why many players expect a GTA 6 PC port eventually. It is a reasonable expectation. It is not a confirmation, and Rockstar has changed its approach between games before.

### Why Rockstar launches on console first

The practical argument is straightforward. Consoles are fixed hardware, so a team can tune a huge open world for exactly two target machines before worrying about the endless variety of PC setups. Zelnick has framed it as serving the core audience first. Some former Rockstar developers have described it the same way: lock down the console experience, then build the PC version knowing what the game can do. So, will gta 6 come to pc after launch?

## <Color value="#ff7a45">The GTA 6 PC release date rumor: reported, not confirmed</Color>

### The early-2027 report

<Figure src="https://res.cloudinary.com/dd3sljpn/image/upload/f_auto,q_auto/v1795523999/gtawiki/will-gta-6-come-to-pc-after-launch/hehcxae8mtgyroifwnc2.webp" alt="GTA 6 PC release date rumor summary noting the unconfirmed Q1 2027 GamesBeat report and lack of Rockstar confirmation" caption="An October 2026 report pointed to a Q1 2027 window, though Rockstar has not confirmed any PC release date." />

On October 13, 2026, GamesBeat reported that Rockstar plans to release GTA 6 on PC in the first quarter of 2027, citing four industry sources who were not named. Rockstar did not respond to the report, and neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has confirmed it. This is the origin of most “GTA 6 PC 2027” headlines you will see.

### How much weight to give it

Everyone has one question that - **will gta 6 come to pc after launch**? Anonymous-source reports can be right, and this outlet is not a rumor mill. Still, a first-quarter 2027 release would land roughly two to four months after the console launch, shorter than any gap in the table above. That does not make the report wrong. It means the sensible position is to wait for Rockstar rather than plan around the rumor.

Rockstar has released no PC build, beta, demo or pre-alpha. If a file claims to be one, do not run it.
