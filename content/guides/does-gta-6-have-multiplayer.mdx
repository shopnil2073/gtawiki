Does GTA 6 have multiplayer? Not at launch. Grand Theft Auto VI came out on November 19, 2026 as a single-player game, with no online mode, no co-op and no second-player option. Rockstar has not announced a GTA 6 online mode, a name for one, or a release date.

If you were expecting a new GTA Online to be waiting on day one, that is a surprise, and it has left a lot of players with the same three questions: is a GTA 6 multiplayer mode coming, when, and what can you play with friends in the meantime? This guide separates what Rockstar has confirmed from what is only reported or rumored, using the facts as of November 24, 2026.

## Does GTA 6 have multiplayer at launch? The short answer

No. Rockstar’s June pre-order announcement described Grand Theft Auto VI as a single-player experience, and the company repeated that description in a statement to IGN. The PlayStation and Xbox store listings say the same, and Rockstar’s launch-day post talks about the story, the soundtrack and the launch bonuses without mentioning an online mode at all.

### What “single-player experience” rules out

- No online lobbies, sessions or matchmaking
- No co-op missions and no local second player
- No competitive modes
- No crossplay between PS5 and Xbox, because there is no online play to cross
- No private or community servers, because Rockstar has released no multiplayer component to host
- No FiveM-style multiplayer framework, which exists for GTA V on PC but has not been announced for GTA 6

### Two main characters, one player

GTA 6 follows two leads, Lucia and Jason, and it is easy to read that as a built-in co-op setup. It is not. Having two protagonists is a storytelling choice. Rockstar calls the game single-player, and nothing from the company suggests a second human player can join.

## GTA 6 online release: what is reported versus what is confirmed

### The 2027 reports

The only timing that has circulated comes from outside Rockstar. In a Bloomberg interview in September, Twitch chief executive Dan Clancy suggested GTA 6’s multiplayer would launch in 2027. In October, Bloomberg’s Jason Schreier reported that the multiplayer mode was expected in 2027, and a later Bloomberg video put it at roughly a year after the main game, which would mean late 2027. Rockstar has confirmed none of this, so “GTA 6 online release 2027” is a credible report, not a date.

### Rumors to treat with caution

- **“It arrives a month after launch”:** this claim is repeated widely, but no Rockstar source stands behind it.
- **Player counts such as 64 or 96:** these come from a 2025 rumor about internal server tests and have never been confirmed.

### What Rockstar’s earlier games suggest

Rockstar has a history of launching online play separately from the story. Grand Theft Auto V arrived in September 2013, and GTA Online followed about two weeks later, on October 1, with a bumpy first few weeks. Red Dead Redemption 2 launched in October 2018, and Red Dead Online opened as a public beta roughly a month after that.

Both of those online modes had been announced about a month before the single-player game came out. GTA 6 launched with no announcement at all, which tells us only that the plan, whatever it is, is different or later this time.

## Why Rockstar may have held GTA 6 multiplayer back

Rockstar has not explained why the answer to “does GTA 6 have multiplayer” is no at launch, so everything in this section is informed speculation:

- **Story first:** launching one polished single-player game keeps attention on the campaign and the world.
- **Server stability:** GTA Online’s rocky start in 2013 is well remembered, and a bigger game means a bigger launch risk.
- **A separate rollout:** a GTA 6 multiplayer mode can be built, tested and announced on its own timeline, with its own marketing moment.

## Can you play GTA 6 co-op or with friends?

Not in GTA 6. There is no co-op mode, no split screen and no way to bring a friend into your game. If you want to play with other people today, your options sit outside this game:

- **GTA Online in GTA V:** still running on consoles and PC, with sessions of up to 30 players.
- **Play GTA 6 together without co-op:** hop on a voice call or share your screen while one person holds the controller.
- **FiveM roleplay servers:** these are community servers for GTA V on PC. They are not available for GTA 6.

## GTA Online on GTA V is still running

The older multiplayer mode has not gone anywhere. Take-Two has said Rockstar will keep supporting GTA Online with new content and events while GTA 6 is out, and no shutdown or end date has been announced. If you want Rockstar multiplayer right now, that is the place to find it.

Rockstar has also not said whether any progress, characters, vehicles or purchases from GTA Online would carry into a future GTA 6 online mode, so do not spend money on GTA Online expecting it to transfer.
