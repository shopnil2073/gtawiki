## First delay: from fall 2025 to May 26, 2026

In May 2025, Rockstar announced that the game needed more time and set a new date of May 26, 2026. The second trailer arrived shortly after, on May 6, 2025, and repeated the new date.

## Second delay: from May 26 to November 19, 2026

On November 6, 2025, Rockstar posted a short message saying the game would now release on November 19, 2026. The studio apologized for the added wait and said the extra months would let it finish the game with the level of polish players expect.

## Did the game launch on time?

Yes. After the second delay the date held. Rockstar published a launch trailer on October 15, 2026, pre-loads opened on November 10, and the game released on November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
